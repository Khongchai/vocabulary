Get these connectors, bruh.

AI Explanations for why

```
Because those words are the infrastructure of the language.

Most beginners focus on nouns:

apple
table
train
restaurant

…but fluency mostly comes from:

connecting ideas,
managing conversation,
expressing relationships between thoughts.

That list gives you exactly that.

For example, with only ~20–30 content words plus those structures, you can already say hundreds of things.

Example

Without connectors:

Yo estudio español. Estoy ocupado. Me gusta aprender idiomas.

Sounds robotic and disconnected.

With connectors:

Estudio español aunque estoy ocupado porque me gusta aprender idiomas.

Suddenly:

causal reasoning,
contrast,
personality,
nuance,
natural flow.

That’s a massive jump from very few words.

Why the verbs matter

Those verbs are extremely high-frequency and combinatorial.

Take just querer.

You instantly unlock:

quiero estudiar
quiero viajar
quiero hablar
quiero aprender
quiero comer
quiero descansar

One verb + infinitives = dozens of usable sentences.

Same with:

tener que
poder
ir a
pensar
hacer

These are “language multipliers.”

Why time phrases matter

Without time, conversation is impossible.

You need:

yesterday
tomorrow
always
never
sometimes

Otherwise every sentence exists in a weird eternal present.

Even basic storytelling requires them:

Ayer trabajé mucho.
Mañana voy a estudiar.
Siempre programo por la noche.
```
